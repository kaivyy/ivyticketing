# Participant Onboarding Guide

Welcome to IvyTicketing. This guide walks you through setting up your account, discovering events, completing race registration, and accessing your race materials.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Navigate to the IvyTicketing homepage or the registration portal of your event.
2. Click **Sign Up** / **Register**.
3. Provide your:
   - Full legal name (as printed on your passport or national ID card)
   - Email address
   - Mobile phone number
   - Secure password
4. Submit the registration form. You will be logged in immediately with your session token securely stored.

---

## Step 2: Complete Your Athlete Profile

Having an accurate athlete profile ensures smooth registration during high-demand sales and accurate emergency communications.

Navigate to **Account Profile** and verify:
- **Emergency Contact**: Name and international phone number (`+62...`).
- **Date of Birth and Gender**: Essential for correct age category placement and timing rankings.
- **T-Shirt / Jersey Size**: Selected according to the event's apparel sizing chart.
- **Medical Notes**: Any drug allergies or relevant medical conditions for race-day medics.

---

## Step 3: Browse and Select an Event

1. Explore the public catalog at `/events` or visit your organizer's dedicated portal.
2. Review event details, route maps, elevation profiles, and registration opening schedules.
3. Select your desired race category (e.g. 42K Marathon, 21K Half Marathon, 10K).

---

## Step 4: Register According to the Event Mode

The event will indicate its active registration mode:

- **Direct Registration**: Proceed directly to fill out registration forms and complete checkout.
- **War Queue**: If high traffic is active, you will be placed into the waiting room. Keep your browser tab open. Do not refresh. When your turn arrives, you will automatically be directed to checkout with a 5-minute reservation timer.
- **Ballot (Lottery)**: Submit your entry during the application window without upfront payment. Check your email when results are drawn to see if you have won an entry grant.

---

## Step 5: Complete Payment

1. Review your order summary, breakdown of ticket prices, administrative fees, and any applied coupon discounts.
2. Choose your preferred payment method:
   - **QRIS**: Scan the dynamic QR code using any banking or e-wallet application (GoPay, OVO, Dana, BCA Mobile).
   - **Virtual Account**: Copy the 16-digit bank transfer number.
   - **Credit / Debit Card**: Enter card credentials secured with 3D-Secure OTP.
3. Complete payment before the 15-minute expiration countdown expires.

---

## Step 6: Access Your Digital Ticket

Once payment is confirmed:
1. Navigate to **My Tickets** in your dashboard.
2. Your ticket will show `Status: VALID` with a cryptographic HMAC QR code.
3. Save or screenshot the QR code for racepack pickup and gate entry.
